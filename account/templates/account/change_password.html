Password Requirements

To create an ultra-secure password, ensure it satisfies these criteria:

  • Minimum 8 characters in length
  • At least one uppercase letter (A-Z)
  • At least one lowercase letter (a-z)
  • At least one number (0-9)
  • At least one special symbol (!@#$%^&*)
Security Best Practices
  • Never share your KiddoCore login credentials with classmates or unauthorized individuals.
  • Avoid using personal names, dates of birth, or predictable phone numbers.
  • Your session on this device will remain authenticated after updating your password.
